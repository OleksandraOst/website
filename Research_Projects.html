Environmental Protection and Satellites

As a Science-Policy Research Assistant at UBC and Junior Fellow at OSI I estimated atmospheric pollution from satellites and debris re-entries. Also, I worked on regulations of light and radio pollution to protect the skies for astronomy and mankind. This science-policy research resulted in 2 publications.

Research on Blazars

Time series analysis examines how data evolves over time. In this project, I analyzed time series data from blazars, a type of active galactic nucleus where a supermassive black hole emits high-energy particle jets directed toward Earth. To better understand their variability, I applied concepts from chaos theory to study the complex dynamics of these systems.
